## Two versions of the future

Shenzhen has opened over seven hundred kilometres of road to autonomous testing, across more than a dozen companies. And a few kilometres away, the same sensors and chips are loose in trays, sold by the handful. It is the only city where you can do both in the same afternoon.

![An autonomous vehicle on Binhe Boulevard in Futian, Shenzhen](https://cdn.lyriktrip.com/s/bbaa723bc8c9.webp)

## How it unfolds

13:50 · The district that took the driver out

This matters and most write-ups get it wrong. In much of Shenzhen an autonomous taxi still carries a safety driver. Pingshan is the district that opened its full road network and went to commercial operation with nobody in the car at all — which is why the ride happens here and not somewhere more convenient.

![An Apollo Go robotaxi waiting at the kerb](https://cdn.lyriktrip.com/s/4d2a05a52bfb.webp)

14:20 · Watch what the car thinks it sees

The cabin screen shows the world as the system has it: every pedestrian a box, every vehicle a box, lane lines it has decided are real. Watching it hesitate at a scooter that hasn't committed to a turn tells you more about the state of autonomy than any specification does.

![The roof sensor unit of a robotaxi at close range](https://cdn.lyriktrip.com/s/decff108da70.webp)

14:50 · It gives way to the delivery rider

The published test runs are specific about what these cars handle: pulling round a delivery scooter parked in a live lane, yielding to pedestrians and road workers crossing, and completing unprotected left turns, lane changes and U-turns. None of that is dramatic to watch. That is the point — the achievement is being boring in traffic.

![A driverless shuttle giving way at a crossing](https://cdn.lyriktrip.com/s/ff65f979b012.webp)

15:30 · The same future, sold by the tray

Huaqiangbei trades a serious share of the world's electronics, in a building layout closer to a fabric market than a mall. Components in trays, finished devices in cases, and a working knowledge of supply chains behind every counter. Your guide's job here is to keep you out of the tourist aisles.

![The Sundan electronics store on Huaqiangbei](https://cdn.lyriktrip.com/s/7ecd975765e1.webp)

16:10 · Translators, glasses, and things that walk

AI translation earpieces, smart glasses, quadruped and humanoid toys, drones of every size. Some of it is remarkable and some is junk, and half the value of the hour is learning to tell which is which by handling it — a skill nobody acquires from a product page.

![The Huaqiangbei pedestrian street between electronics towers](https://cdn.lyriktrip.com/s/f142604316c4.webp)

## One is the future finished. The other is the futurein parts .

The point of doing both

The robotaxi is the polished end: a closed system, enormous capital, full redundancy in the drive-by-wire, and a ride so smooth the technology disappears.

Huaqiangbei is the open end: the same sensors, the same chips, loose in trays, being bought by people who will make something else out of them by Friday.

Standing in both on one afternoon is the clearest explanation of this city anyone has come up with. It is also why the afternoon runs in this order.
