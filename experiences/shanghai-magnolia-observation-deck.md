## Questions

Where do I go, and what do I see?

The roof of Magnolia Plaza, 501 Dongdaming Road, Hongkou. Metro Line 12, International Cruise Terminal, Exit 3, then basement one. Open air, 320 metres. Opposite: Shanghai Tower, the World Financial Center, the Oriental Pearl and the Huangpu. There is a stage on the deck. The album shows an opera performer there.

Why not Wujiaochang?

One ticket page writes Wujiaochang and Magnolia Plaza together. The government address for this North Bund tower is 501 Dongdaming Road. Follow Line 12.

Is it only at night?

The slot sold here is the evening. On 24 Oct 2026 the starts were 19:30, 20:30 and 21:30. Daytime hours were published when it opened in 2025. We confirm whether they still run when you book.

Does someone lead the visit?

No guide on site. English advice before the trip and a digital guide are included.

Are drinks in the price?

No. \$82 per person is the ticket. Meals, pickup and an on-site guide are not included.

How is this different from Shanghai Tower?

Shanghai Tower is in Pudong, floor 118, 546 metres, behind glass. This is a Puxi roof, 320 metres, open air, with those towers opposite you. They are different tickets.

Shanghai Tower or Jin Mao Tower: which observation deck should you choose?

Shanghai Tower is in Pudong—118 floors, 546 meters, viewed through glass. This spot is on a rooftop in Puxi, 320 meters up, fully exposed to the sky, with the tower directly across from you. The two tickets are not for the same experience.
