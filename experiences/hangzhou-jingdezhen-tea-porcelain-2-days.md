## Chinese tea and porcelain — have you seen where they come from ?

For centuries, what China sold the world came down to three things: tea, silk and porcelain — English even calls porcelain “china”. But most of us meet them in supermarket tea bags and shop windows. Over these two days we take you to the source. Day one is Hangzhou: walk into the tea hills of Longjing Village and pick tea with the growers, then follow the shore of West Lake at an easy pace. Day two is Jingdezhen, where porcelain has been fired for more than a thousand years: sit down at the wheel, pull a lump of clay into a bowl, paint your own design on it and send it to the kiln. What you bring home isn't something from a souvenir shop. It's a piece of China that belongs only to you.

## Tea, silk, porcelain — one at a time, with your own hands

Beyond tea, silk and porcelain, an evening on Hefang Street lets you eat your way along these Hangzhou snacks.

Signature

![Tea pickers in conical hats among the terraced tea rows of Longjing Village](https://cdn.lyriktrip.com/s/2ef5afcdd971.jpg)

Pick tea in Longjing Village

Head up the tea hills with the growers and learn to take only the one bud and one leaf. Longjing is picked in spring, summer and autumn, so you don't have to come in spring.

![An embroidered silk dragon robe](https://cdn.lyriktrip.com/s/3abf13d258de.jpg)

See Hangzhou's silk

Hangzhou has been a capital of silk since ancient times. At the silk museum, see how a single strand of silkworm silk becomes sumptuous brocade and dragon robes.

Signature

![A pair of hands throwing clay on a potter's wheel](https://cdn.lyriktrip.com/s/2b6fce3acca7.jpg)

Throw a piece yourself

On the wheel, pull a lump of clay into a bowl or a cup. A teacher steadies your hands alongside — and if it comes out crooked, that's fine, that's your shape.

Signature

![Painting blue-and-white decoration on a bisque tea bowl](https://cdn.lyriktrip.com/s/44c852c93c76.jpg)

Paint your own design

Paint blue-and-white decoration on a bisque piece, or write your name, or draw a cat. Once it's fired, it's one of a kind in the world.

![Freshly made congbaogui in a paper bag](https://cdn.lyriktrip.com/s/26bd98eb91d3.jpg)

Congbaogui

A thin crepe rolled around fried dough and spring onion, pressed crisp on a griddle, then brushed with sweet bean sauce — a street snack from around Hefang Street.

![Pink dingsheng cakes stamped “dingsheng” in a steamer](https://cdn.lyriktrip.com/s/3f8df1f4c617.jpg)

Dingsheng cake

A pink steamed rice-flour cake stamped with the characters “dingsheng”, meaning “certain victory”. Soft and lightly sweet.

![Lotus root powder topped with osmanthus on a wooden tray](https://cdn.lyriktrip.com/s/3b5b19321723.jpg)

Osmanthus West Lake lotus root powder

Lotus root starch whisked into a translucent bowlful and scattered with golden osmanthus. Warming and gently sweet.

## Your itinerary

Timings below assume a Shanghai departure and are for reference; actual train times depend on the tickets issued. Departures from Zhejiang are adjusted to your starting point.

### Day 1

07:30 · Leave your hotel for Hangzhou (Shanghai example)

A private car collects you from your hotel and takes you to Shanghai Hongqiao Station, with your guide travelling alongside. The high-speed train reaches Hangzhou East Station in about 1 hour. Departures from Zhejiang are arranged around your starting point.

11:00 · Pick tea in Longjing Village, and eat tea

Head up the tea hills with the growers to pick tea, then have lunch in the village — a meal with tea worked into the dishes.

![A tea table set on the hillside, a cup of Longjing with a few tea snacks](https://cdn.lyriktrip.com/s/bff55c0f9133.jpg)

14:00 · A walk beside West Lake

An afternoon strolling slowly along the lakeshore.

![The lakeshore beneath the willows along West Lake's Bai Causeway](https://cdn.lyriktrip.com/s/ccf14431a203.jpg)

17:30 · Hefang Street: silk and Hangzhou snacks

Visit the silk museum to see Hangzhou silk, then eat Hangzhou snacks on the old street as dinner, and back to the hotel to rest around 19:30.

![Old shopfronts on Hangzhou's Hefang Street](https://cdn.lyriktrip.com/s/e1a46c48a0af.jpg)

### Day 2

07:30 · High-speed rail to Jingdezhen

From Hangzhou West Station it's about 3 hours to Jingdezhen North Station, where you'll try a few local snacks on arrival (snacks, not a full meal).

![Blue-and-white porcelain vases on the grass at the Jingdezhen Ancient Kiln Folk Customs Museum](https://cdn.lyriktrip.com/s/98d305ad0832.jpg)

13:00 · Throw, paint, and into the kiln

A teacher takes you through throwing and painting, and your work goes into the kiln. Firing in Jingdezhen generally takes two to four weeks.

![Bisque pieces drying on wooden racks in a Jingdezhen workshop](https://cdn.lyriktrip.com/s/4caeb195679c.jpg)

17:30 · High-speed rail home

About 4 hours, arriving at Shanghai Hongqiao around 21:30, where a private car returns you to your hotel.

## Frequently asked questions

Can we take the porcelain we make in Jingdezhen home with us?

Your piece has to be fired, which generally takes two to four weeks, so you can't collect the finished work the same day. If you'd like the fired piece sent home, talk to us separately about shipping.

I've never done pottery. Will it turn out all right?

Yes. A teacher takes you through throwing and painting hand over hand, and if your first attempt comes out a little lopsided, that's exactly what makes it one of a kind.

Can we still pick tea in Longjing Village if it isn't spring?

Yes. Longjing is picked in spring, summer and autumn, so there are close to two hundred days a year when picking is possible.

Can we start from Zhejiang?

Yes. We depart from either Shanghai or Zhejiang and pick you up at your hotel; train times and departure times are rearranged around where you're starting from, and the times on this page assume a Shanghai departure.

Why does the price per person go down when more people come?

The private car, guide and local transport are arranged for the group as a whole, so the more people travelling together, the less each person pays. Five or more get the best per-person price.

What kind of hotel is it, and how is a solo room calculated?

A four-star hotel in Hangzhou, twin room. Staying alone means paying a single-occupancy supplement — please contact us to confirm.

What language does the guide speak?

English and Chinese guides are provided as standard; German, French, Spanish, Italian and other languages need to be arranged in advance when you book.

English and Chinese-speaking guides are available by default. For guides in other languages such as German, French, Spanish, or Italian, please let us know in advance when booking.
