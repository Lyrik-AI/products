## The closed city

Most industrial tourism here is a corridor and a window. Someone else operates; you nod. This store was built the other way round. Eight hundred square metres, and the founding idea was a single word: experience. Which means a demo bay, a hands-on floor and a flight cinema — and things you are expected to pick up.

![The flagship store building seen from the plaza](https://cdn.lyriktrip.com/s/7cbbb4c4c978.webp)

## How it unfolds

09:50 · Opened December 2015, and the first

This was DJI's first flagship store anywhere in the world, on the southeast corner of OCT Bay next to the carnival plaza. Inside: a static display area, a flight demonstration bay, a hands-on zone, a talks area and a flight cinema. It opens ten in the morning until ten at night, later at weekends, and does not close.

![A drone on display inside the flagship store](https://cdn.lyriktrip.com/s/fc87a7e812ec.webp)

10:20 · Two seconds to understand

A gimbal is the fastest way to feel what all of this is doing. You hold it, you walk badly on purpose, and the image simply refuses to move. Three motors and an accelerometer, arguing with your arm several hundred times a second, and winning.

![Hands holding a camera gimbal](https://cdn.lyriktrip.com/s/a2e741347054.webp)

10:50 · Let go of the sticks and watch

The demonstration bay is enclosed, so a first-timer can fly without anything going badly. The instructive bit is not flying it — it is releasing the sticks. The aircraft stops dead in the air and holds, correcting against its own drift, and you can watch it deciding.

![The darkened experience zone inside the store](https://cdn.lyriktrip.com/s/d6bf932f0ec0.webp)

11:30 · It appears from the bottom up

A printer nozzle lays a line about a tenth of a millimetre thick, then does it again, a few thousand times. Nothing about the process is hidden — you watch the whole object come into existence — and it is far slower and far more absorbing than anyone expects.

![A 3D printer nozzle laying down filament](https://cdn.lyriktrip.com/s/500c4e1f28c9.webp)

12:10 · Chosen on screen, lifted off warm

You pick or adjust the object on screen first, then lever it off the plate while it is still warm — a small and genuinely satisfying moment. It is also the neatest possible souvenir: chosen by you, made in the city that makes them, and small enough for hand luggage.

![A three-dimensional model on a laptop screen at the printing centre](https://cdn.lyriktrip.com/s/6ecc23a0cfee.webp)
