## China Ready

### 🔐 VPN & Internet Access

<sub>Do this before you fly</sub>

- **What's blocked:** Google, Gmail, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, and most Western news sites are inaccessible in China without a VPN.
- **Download before you leave:** It's nearly impossible to download a VPN once inside China. Install and test at home. Recommended: Astrill (most reliable), Mullvad, ExpressVPN — subscribe to at least two in case one stops working.
- **Hotel Wi-Fi tip:** Some hotels block VPN connections on their Wi-Fi. Use your phone's mobile data as a hotspot if the VPN drops.
- **Good news:** WhatsApp works perfectly through a VPN, so your messaging stays unchanged. Apple iMessage and Signal also work.

### 💳 Paying in China

<sub>Your concierge sets this up on Day 1</sub>

- **China is cashless:** Alipay and WeChat Pay are used for almost everything — restaurants, taxis, markets, tickets. Cash is rarely accepted in cities.
- **International cards now work:** Foreign Visa/Mastercard can now link directly to Alipay (set up in minutes, \$5,000 single transaction limit). Your concierge walks you through the setup on your first morning.
- **Carry some cash:** We recommend ¥500–800 RMB in cash for the rare vendor that won't accept digital payment, and for small towns.

## Before you book — Thin Air, Then Sea Level FAQ

### I have a UK passport and enter China visa-free. Can I still get a Tibet permit?

### Can I do this trip in October, November or December 2026?

Not the full 18 days, and we would rather say that than sell you a shortened version quietly. The plateau section is arranged from April to October only, and the river ships end their 2026 season on November 27 and December 7. Because this is an 18-day plan, a departure after mid-October crosses into November on the plateau. Put the two windows together and nothing in October to December fits end to end. As designed, this journey sells from April to October. If your dates are fixed in that quarter, the three-city classic run at the front of this plan does work then, and we will price that instead rather than bend this one.

### Is 3,650 meters going to make me ill? 3,650

Some guests feel it and some do not, and nobody can tell you in advance which one you are. What the plan does about it: the ascent happens in one flight on day 8, and the whole of day 9 carries nothing at all. Every day after that carries a single item rather than three. The highest point of the route is the city you sleep in rather than a mountain pass, and the descent is by river over three nights instead of a second flight. ### Why come down by boat instead of just flying to Shanghai?
