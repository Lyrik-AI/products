## Overview

This 3-day itinerary begins in Chengdu, connecting Shuangqiao Valley in the Four Sisters Mountains, Bipeng Valley, and Dagu Glacier into a cohesive plateau route. On Day 1, the journey passes through Yingxiu and Cat's Rib Ridge before entering Shuangqiao Valley to admire snow-capped peaks, alpine meadows, grasslands, and forests, ending with an overnight stay in Li County. The driver-guide handles all transfers, hotel check-ins, and provides brief introductions along the way.

## Day-by-day itinerary

### Day 1 · Chengdu - Maoliang Ridge - Shuangqiaogou - Lixian

*View the snow-capped mountains from Maoniling Ridge, then explore Shuangqiao Valley.*

**Departing from Chengdu**

Pickup within the 3rd Ring Road of Chengdu, followed by a drive via the Duwen Expressway to Yingxiu, passing through Wolong and the Balang Mountain Tunnel.

**Cat Nose Ridge Overlook**

Stop for about 10 minutes to capture panoramic views of the Siguniang Mountains.

**Shuangqiao Valley**

Follow the valley to see snow-capped peaks, pastures, meadows, forests, as well as the Yin-Yang Valley, Hunter Peak, Sun-Moon Treasure Mirror, Five-Color Mountain Sea-Buckthorn Forest Trail, Cow Shed, and Ox-Heart Mountain.

**Head to Lixian**

Head to Lixian County via Lixiao Road, enjoying views of snow-capped mountains, forests, river valleys, and alpine meadows along the way. The route may be diverted due to road closures. Check into your hotel and rest.

<sub>• **Accommodation:** Lixian Hotel</sub>

<sub>• **Dining:** Meals on this day are self-arranged</sub>

### Day 2 · Li County - Bipenggou - Heishui County

*Spend a full day in Bipenggou exploring forests, alpine lakes, waterfalls, and snow-capped mountains.*

**Head to Bibengou**

After breakfast, depart from Li County and take the sightseeing bus into Bipenggou.

**In-Valley Route**

Walk along the primeval forest and streams, and enjoy spring blossoms, summer shade, autumn foliage, or hot springs with snow views, depending on the season.

**Zhuoma Lake and Bailong Waterfall**

Journey through pristine forests along the ancient Tea Horse Road, visiting Zhuoma Lake, Bailong Waterfall, Moon Bay, and Panyang Lake.

**Head to Heishui County**

After the tour, proceed to Heishui County and check in at the hotel for rest.

<sub>• **Accommodation:** Hotel in Heishui County</sub>

<sub>🍽️ **Meals:** Hotel breakfast included; lunch and dinner are on your own.</sub>

### Day 3 · Heishui - Dagu Glacier - Chengdu

*Visit a Tibetan village and a glacial lake before returning to Chengdu.*

**Enter Dagu Glacier**

After breakfast, head to Dagu Glacier Scenic Area and take the sightseeing bus to enter.

**Dagu Tibetan Village and Dagu Lake**

Along the way, visit Golden Monkey Lake, the Tibetan Folk Customs Experience Area, the Upper, Middle, and Lower Dagu Tibetan Villages, the Fish Release Lake, the Alpine Pastures, and Dagu Lake.

**Glacier No. 1**

Take the sightseeing cable car up to the glacier's heart at 4,850 meters above sea level, and spend about 4 hours exploring. You can also choose to see Lingyun Waterfall and the Red Rock Slope.

**Return to Chengdu**

After the tour, return to Chengdu by coach, concluding the itinerary.

## China Ready

We've solved the hard parts. Here's everything a first-time Western traveler needs to know before arriving.

### 🔐 VPN & Internet Access

<sub>Do this before you fly</sub>

- **What's blocked:** Google, Gmail, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, and most Western news sites are inaccessible in China without a VPN.
- **Download before you leave:** It's nearly impossible to download a VPN once inside China. Install and test at home. Recommended: Astrill (most reliable), Mullvad, ExpressVPN — subscribe to at least two in case one stops working.
- **Hotel Wi-Fi tip:** Some hotels block VPN connections on their Wi-Fi. Use your phone's mobile data as a hotspot if the VPN drops.
- **Good news:** WhatsApp works perfectly through a VPN, so your messaging stays unchanged. Apple iMessage and Signal also work.

### 💳 Paying in China

<sub>Your concierge sets this up on Day 1</sub>

- **China is cashless:** Alipay and WeChat Pay are used for almost everything — restaurants, taxis, markets, tickets. Cash is rarely accepted in cities.
- **International cards now work:** Foreign Visa/Mastercard can now link directly to Alipay (set up in minutes, \$5,000 single transaction limit). Your concierge walks you through the setup on your first morning.
- **Carry some cash:** We recommend ¥500–800 RMB in cash for the rare vendor that won't accept digital payment, and for small towns.
- **Booking this trip:** Your deposit and balance are charged via Stripe — the same trusted platform used by Airbnb and Booking.com. USD billing, no surprise conversion fees.

### 🛂 Visa & Entry

<sub>We handle the paperwork</sub>

- **Do you need a visa?:** Most US, UK, EU, Australian, Canadian, and New Zealand passport holders need a tourist (L) visa for stays over 10 days. We provide a visa invitation letter with every booking — it significantly speeds up approval.
- **240-hour visa-free transit:** Citizens of 55+ countries (including USA, UK, most of EU, AUS, NZ, Canada) can enter China visa-free for up to 10 days if transiting through eligible ports. This covers our 12-day itinerary only if you have a confirmed onward flight.
- **Processing time:** Apply 4–6 weeks before departure. Standard processing: 4–7 business days. Express (3 days) and same-day options available at most Chinese consulates.
- **What we provide:** Visa invitation letter, hotel confirmation for all nights, suggested itinerary document — everything the consulate needs, formatted correctly.

### 🛡️ Safety & Practical Tips

<sub>Our responsibility, always</sub>

- **Is China safe?:** China consistently ranks among the safest countries for foreign tourists. Violent crime against visitors is extremely rare. Cities have extensive CCTV coverage and a visible police presence.
